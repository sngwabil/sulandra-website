A. External accounting consultant
B. Director of Operations (DOO) / COO
C. Part-time receptionist

C. Lock all home files until legal counsel arrives
D. Discharge non-compliant clients before review

A. Schedules are standardized strictly for staff convenience
B. Services, community integration, and routines reflect individual choices and ISP outcomes
