Website Privacy Notice

Effective date: August 2, 2026

This notice explains how Sulandra Health may collect, use, protect, and retain information submitted through its public website.

Do not submit emergency or highly sensitive information through the public website. Call 911 for an emergency. Do not include Social Security numbers, detailed medical records, diagnoses, medication lists, financial account numbers, or government identification numbers in a general website form.

Information we may collect

We may collect contact information, general service interests, employment application information, technical website data, and communications voluntarily submitted to us.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate service availability, process employment applications, operate and secure the website, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

Information may be shared with authorized Sulandra personnel and service providers that support website hosting, communications, recruiting, security, or business operations. Information is not sold for advertising purposes.

Security and retention

Sulandra uses administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the type of information involved. No internet transmission is completely risk-free. Records are retained according to operational, legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements.

Your choices

You may request correction of inaccurate contact information or ask questions about this notice by contacting Sulandra Health through its official business contact channels.

Updates

This notice may be updated when practices, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
