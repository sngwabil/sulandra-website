How this works: Complete the guided questions below. The system will place your answers on the current official IRS Form W-4, generate a PDF, and show it to you for review. Nothing is submitted until you approve the completed PDF.

Step 1 — Personal information

Enter your name exactly as it appears on your Social Security card.

Step 2 — Multiple jobs or spouse works

Check this only when there are exactly two jobs total and the option is also selected on the other job’s Form W-4. For more complex situations, use the IRS estimator.

Step 3 — Dependents and other credits

For 2026, multiply qualifying children under age 17 by $2,200 and other dependents by $500, then add other eligible credits.

Step 4 — Other adjustments

Leave a field at zero when it does not apply. Do not include wages from jobs in “other income.”

Step 5 — Sign

Review your completed official Form W-4

Review every field carefully. Use “Go back and edit” when anything is incorrect. Submit only after the PDF is accurate.

Your Social Security number is used only to complete the official W-4 PDF. The system stores the signed PDF and only the last four digits in the structured record. The completed PDF is available to authorized Human Resources administrators and is added to the applicant/employee folder.
