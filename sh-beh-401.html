Section 2: Action Demonstrations in De-escalation

Review the side-by-side action demonstrations comparing reactive escalation handling against Sulandra Health de-escalation gold standards:

❌ Reactive / Punitive Response

Raising voice, arguing over minor rules, pointing fingers, and crowding an agitated resident's personal space.

✔ Sulandra Gold Standard De-escalation

Maintaining a calm, low vocal tone, stepping back to provide physical space, validating feelings, and offering simple choices.

Section 4: Comprehensive Case Study – Post-Crisis Recovery

Case Background: Following an acute behavioral outburst in the dining area, a resident is now quiet and sitting alone in their room, looking exhausted. A staff member approaches wanting to lecture the resident about their inappropriate behavior during dinner.

Analysis: Lecturing or rehashing arguments during the post-crisis recovery phase re-triggers agitation. Under Sulandra Health protocols, staff must allow quiet time for emotional recovery, restore normal routines gently, and conduct supportive staff debriefs away from the individual.
