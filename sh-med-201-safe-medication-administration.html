Section 2: Safe Medication-Pass Actions

Medication must remain secured and under the authorized staff member’s direct control throughout preparation and administration.

Unsafe practice

Preparing medication and leaving it unattended while responding to another task.

Sulandra safety practice

Verify against the MAR, maintain direct control, observe administration as required, and document immediately after the dose is administered.

Section 4: Controlled-Substance Discrepancy

Case: During shift handoff, the physical controlled-substance count differs from the signed count sheet by one dose.

Required response: Do not alter the count or attempt to reconcile it privately. Secure the medication, notify the RN or designated supervisor immediately, preserve records, and follow incident-reporting and investigation procedures.

Additional high-risk situations requiring prompt escalation include suspected adverse drug reactions, wrong medication or dose, missed doses, medication unavailable, vomiting after administration, and any unexpected change in condition.

For anaphylaxis, severe breathing difficulty, unresponsiveness, or another life-threatening emergency, activate emergency services and follow the individual’s emergency plan.
