Rehabilitation Services

Rehabilitation needs can be discussed during intake so the care team can determine what support, coordination, or referral options are appropriate for the individual’s goals and authorized plan.

Goal-Oriented Support

Rehabilitation-related goals may involve mobility, daily functioning, communication, safety, independence, or recovery support.

Care Coordination

When specialized licensed rehabilitation services are required, the intake team can identify whether Sulandra provides the requested service directly or whether coordination with another provider is needed.

Plan Integration

Approved recommendations can be incorporated into day-to-day support, staff training, and person-centered service planning where appropriate.
