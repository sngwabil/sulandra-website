Behavioral Health Support

Behavioral and emotional-support needs can be discussed during intake so appropriate services, coordination, safeguards, and referral options can be identified.

Person-Centered Planning

Support planning considers communication, triggers, coping strategies, preferences, safety needs, goals, and the person’s existing clinical or behavioral plan.

Team Coordination

When licensed behavioral-health treatment is required, the intake team can determine whether Sulandra provides the requested service directly or coordinate with the individual’s authorized providers.

Staff Support

Where applicable, direct-care staff can be trained on approved behavior-support strategies, crisis-prevention requirements, and person-specific protocols.
