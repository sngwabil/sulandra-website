Companion Care

Companion support focuses on meaningful engagement, routine, community participation, safety, and reducing isolation while respecting the individual’s preferences and independence.

Daily Engagement

Support may include conversation, structured activities, errands, appointments, community access, and participation in preferred routines.

Independence

Companion services are designed to support choice and participation rather than replace abilities the individual can safely use independently.

Flexible Planning

Availability depends on the requested schedule, location, funding source, assessed needs, and applicable service authorization.
