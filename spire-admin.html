SPIRE Administration

SPIRE is the shared Sulandra Health clinical record across every company in the network. Service-home assignments control non-administrator chart access; SPIRE administrators automatically have access to every service home and client chart. Company selection remains relevant only to company-specific operational tools and provenance.
