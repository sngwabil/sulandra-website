System Diagnostics

Capture sanitized evidence such as application, page, workflow, step, action, response, status code, correlation ID, GitHub failure, Railway deployment, integration failure, and timestamps.

Never capture passwords, MFA codes, tokens, raw clinical notes, patient identifiers, SSNs, or unrelated screen content.

Employee Remote Assistance

Support sessions begin only after the employee affirmatively accepts screen sharing in their browser/device.

  1. Open or create the employee's IT ticket.
  2. Send a remote-assistance request.
  3. Employee chooses tab, window, or screen.
  4. A persistent sharing indicator remains visible.
  5. Employee can stop sharing immediately.

Screenshot Evidence

When a still image is useful, the employee explicitly authorizes capture. The screenshot is tied to the ticket and remote-assist session with purpose and consent timestamp.
