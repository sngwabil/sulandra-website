Section 4: Honoring Personal Routines

Case: David prefers to read late and wake later. A relief staff member forces him out of bed at 6:30 AM to match the house schedule, causing agitation.

Analysis: Person-centered care requires staff to adapt support to the individual whenever safely possible. Rigid institutional routines should not replace a person's ordinary preferences, sleep pattern, cultural habits or chosen lifestyle.

Practice expectations: involve individuals in meal planning, household chores, shopping, scheduling, leisure, community activities and interdisciplinary planning. Use prompts, adaptive tools and graduated assistance instead of automatically taking over.
