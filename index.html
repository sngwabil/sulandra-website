How to Start Services

Our team listens to your needs, explains available options, and helps build a support plan that fits the individual and family.

1. Contact Us

Tell us what support you are looking for and the best way to reach you.

2. Consultation

We discuss goals, preferences, schedule, service area, and next steps.

3. Support Plan

We develop a person-centered plan focused on safety, dignity, independence, and family priorities.

4. Begin Services

Once services are authorized and scheduled, our team begins support and continues regular follow-up.
