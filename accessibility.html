Accessibility Statement

Updated: August 2, 2026

Sulandra Health is committed to providing a website that is usable by people with disabilities and compatible with common assistive technologies.

Our approach

We work to provide keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, meaningful headings, descriptive alternative text, sufficient color contrast, responsive layouts, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Website content and features are reviewed as they are added or updated, and identified barriers are prioritized for correction.

Need assistance?

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in another format, contact Sulandra Health through its official business contact channels. Please identify the page, feature, or content involved and describe the format or assistance needed.

Third-party content

Some linked services or embedded third-party resources may be controlled by other organizations. Sulandra Health will work to provide an accessible alternative when reasonably possible.
